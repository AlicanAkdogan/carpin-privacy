CarPin – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18.11.2026

CarPin ("the app") shows widgets such as speed, weather and countdowns on your iPhone and on the CarPlay screen. This policy explains what the app uses and why. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not sell your data.

What the app uses

  • Location. Used while you use the app to show your driving speed and the weather where you are. Your coordinates are sent to Apple Weather (WeatherKit) to get the forecast. We do not store your location on any server of ours.
  • Photos. If you pick a photo for a widget background or for a car in "My Cars", it is stored only on your device. We do not upload it.
  • Camera. Only if you choose to take a new photo for a widget or a car. The photo stays on your device.
  • Car details you enter (name, plate, notes, service date). Stored only on your device.
  • Device identifier. A random identifier is kept in your device's Keychain so that your subscription can be recognised. It is not linked to your name or contacts.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous user ID and purchase information. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Third parties

  • Apple (WeatherKit, App Store, CarPlay).
  • RevenueCat (subscription management).

We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs.

Your choices

You can turn off location, camera or photo access at any time in iOS Settings. Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. You can cancel a subscription in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If we add features that use more data (for example, sharing drawings with a partner), we will update this policy before those features are released.
